Solo Build

Grounded, Not Generated · Built with Claude

preCaution

The Build

Built solo for Anthropic's Built with Claude: Life Sciences hackathon, on the Builder Track, selected as 1 of 500 builders from around 6,000 applications. preCaution reads a free-text experimental protocol and returns a source-linked safety brief specific to that procedure, instead of an 80-page safety data sheet dump. It needs a language model only for reading: comprehension, extraction, and omission-checking across messy prose that a database query cannot parse.

Grounded, Not Generated

Six stages, and Claude appears in three of them, all on the reading side: segmenting the prose into steps, extracting chemicals and vessels, and flagging omissions as advisory. Every safety verdict is produced by the deterministic side: live PubChem lookups for grounding, a hand-encoded interaction table from NOAA CAMEO Chemicals, and pure-Python composition of the brief. Claude reads the protocol. Claude does not write the safety advice.

Why It Matters

The signature demo is a piranha solution protocol, where sodium azide in a later rinse meets an acidic waste stream from four steps earlier and produces hydrazoic acid. No ingredient-list screener catches this, because it only appears when you track which chemicals share a vessel across the prose. That is the case for a protocol-aware reader routed through deterministic, sourced logic.

AI × AI

A Live AI Debate · NTU PACE

Agent vs Agent

The Build

Two AI agents take divergent stances on the same question and debate in real time. React and Vite frontend, the Anthropic API for reasoning, built around a three-layer architecture: a fixed identity per agent, a randomly selected stance per run, and full conversation history passed into every call.

The Insight

Temperature alone did not create meaningful divergence. The randomness that makes each run diverge lives at the prompt level, in the stance modifier appended to each agent's system prompt. The personas, a sceptical COO and an optimistic Chief AI Officer, were placeholders on top of that mechanic.

The Delivery

Presented to a C-suite, Director, and senior-executive audience at an NTU PACE executive leadership workshop. The demo was the proof point for a larger arc: from building systems, to building the bridge between systems and people, to asking what gets built and why.

Kellogg–Morgan Stanley finals

Mekong Resilience Fund

The Challenge

Climate-driven salinity intrusion in Vietnam's Mekong Delta was threatening roughly 1.1 million smallholder farming families and disrupting agricultural supply chains across Southeast Asia. Institutional investors lacked a credible vehicle to fund climate resilience in the region while generating returns.

Strategic Action

Led strategy, narrative architecture, and investor framing for a four-person team. Developed the core Supply Chain Insetting concept, a mechanism where corporations fund climate resilience within their own supply chain geography through outcomes-based success fees, rather than buying unrelated offsets elsewhere. Positioned a $250M blended finance vehicle and presented it to senior investment stakeholders at Morgan Stanley's offices in London.

Business Impact

Reached the global finals as one of 12 teams from 186 globally, and the first Nanyang Business School team to do so. Post-pitch, a judge independently requested the full proposal for further exploration.
